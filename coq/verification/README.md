# Frozen Challenge Verification Harness (RH-Style 3-Step Workflow)

This directory provides a self-contained, reproducible verification harness for the finite-lattice polymer cluster-expansion proofs (including the genuine ordered-tuple $1/n!$ Ursell Kotecký–Preiss tree-graph theorem) and concrete 4D hypercubic lattice ($\mathbb{Z}^4$) plaquette combinatorics and exponential decay in `yang-mills-final-proof/coq/`, modeled after the `Challenge` / `Comparator` / independent-checker workflow used to verify the 2026 Lean 4 Riemann Hypothesis proof. **Target 7**, frozen in the separate file [ChallengeZ2Gauge.v](ChallengeZ2Gauge.v), adds a genuine lattice gauge theory: the 4D $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Wilson theory at strong coupling. Its Wilson-loop area law (**Target 7e**) is frozen in a third file, [ChallengeZ2AreaLaw.v](ChallengeZ2AreaLaw.v). **Target 8**, frozen in a fourth file, [ChallengeSU2Gauge.v](ChallengeSU2Gauge.v), adds a non-abelian lattice gauge theory: the 4D $SU(2)$ Wilson theory (lattice Yang–Mills theory with gauge group $SU(2)$) at strong coupling. **Target 9**, frozen in a fifth file, [ChallengeSU2InfiniteVolume.v](ChallengeSU2InfiniteVolume.v), adds the infinite-volume (thermodynamic) limit of that theory. **Target 10**, frozen in a sixth file, [ChallengeSU2MassGap.v](ChallengeSU2MassGap.v), adds the uniqueness and the lattice symmetries of that infinite-volume state, reflection positivity, and the mass gap of the transfer operator.

> **Honest Scope:** This harness certifies finite-volume abstract polymer graph/metric bounds, conditional cluster-expansion exponential decay, the genuine ordered-tuple $1/n!$ Ursell Kotecký–Preiss (1986) / Penrose (1967) tree-graph theorem (pinned exponential-moment bounds and truncated correlator exponential decay with **zero** analytic assumptions), convergence of the $1/n!$ Mayer/Ursell series to $\ln Z_\Lambda$ for the actual finite-volume compatible-subset partition function $Z_\Lambda$ plus convergence and exponential decay of the exact partition-function log ratio $\ln\frac{Z_\Lambda Z_{\Lambda \setminus (\bar{p}_1 \cup \bar{p}_2)}}{Z_{\Lambda \setminus \bar{p}_1} Z_{\Lambda \setminus \bar{p}_2}}$, and concrete $\mathbb{Z}^4$ plaquette overlap combinatorics (exact 21-neighbor enumeration) plus unconditional finite-volume $\ln Z_\Lambda$ convergence and log-ratio decay for the constant-activity ($\beta > 85$) $\mathbb{Z}^4$ plaquette polymer model. **Target 7** additionally certifies, for the 4D $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Wilson lattice gauge theory in finite volume with free boundary conditions (Wilson action, exact normalized Haar sum over all link configurations): the exact strong-coupling expansion into closed mod-2 surfaces (every $\beta$), exponential clustering of the gauge-invariant connected plaquette correlator for $168|\beta| \le e^{-\mu}$ uniformly in the volume, the exact identification of the partition function with the hard-core polymer partition function of connected closed surfaces (every $\beta$), the Kotecký–Preiss criterion with a convergent Mayer expansion and a volume-uniform bound for the free energy for $189|\tanh\beta| \le 1$, and (Target 7e) the Wilson-loop area-law upper bound $(63|\tanh\beta|)^{ab}\,e^{2(a+b)}$ for $a \times b$ rectangular loops inside the dynamical volume for $63|\tanh\beta| \le 1$, uniformly in the volume. **Target 8** certifies, for the 4D $SU(2)$ Wilson lattice gauge theory in finite volume with free boundary conditions (gauge group = unit quaternions, Wilson action $S(U) = -\beta \sum_{p \in \Lambda} \tfrac12 \mathrm{tr}\, U_p$): exponential clustering of the truncated correlation of any two plaquette-local observables for $168|\beta| \le e^{-\mu}$ (8a) and the Wilson-loop area-law upper bound $(63(e^{2|\beta|}-1))^{ab}\,e^{2(a+b)}$ for $63(e^{2|\beta|}-1) \le 1$ (8b), both uniformly in the volume **and** in the finite a-priori link distribution; the existence of the Gibbs expectation of every bounded uniformly continuous observable with respect to the Haar measure, realized as the limit of the uniform distributions on dyadic grids of the Hopf coordinates of $SU(2) = S^3$ (8c); and the same two bounds for that Haar-measure Gibbs state (8d, 8e). **Target 9** certifies, for the same theory: that enlarging the finite volume changes the expectation of a local observable by an amount that is exponentially small in the distance to the added plaquettes (9a); the existence, for $43008|\beta| \le 1$, of the infinite-volume Haar-measure Gibbs expectation of every bounded uniformly continuous observable localized on finitely many plaquettes, as the limit along the cubes $[-n,n]^4$ (9b); and exponential clustering (9c) and the Wilson-loop area-law bound (9d) for that infinite-volume state. **Target 10** certifies, for that infinite-volume state at $43008|\beta| \le 1$: its independence of the exhausting sequence of finite volumes (10a); its invariance under the lattice translations and the time reflection (10b); reflection positivity $\langle \Theta f \cdot f \rangle \ge 0$ for observables localized at non-negative times (10c); exponential clustering for observables localized on arbitrary finite sets of plaquettes (10d); and the mass gap of the transfer operator $T$ (translation by one unit of time) in the Osterwalder–Schrader form $(F, G) = \langle \Theta F \cdot G \rangle$: with $\varphi = f - \langle f \rangle$ and $\psi = g - \langle g \rangle$ for observables $f, g$ localized at non-negative times, $|(\psi, T^k \psi)| \le e^{-mk} (\psi, \psi)$ (10e) and $(\varphi, T^k \psi)^2 \le e^{-2mk} (\varphi, \varphi)(\psi, \psi)$ (10f), where $m = \mu/2$ whenever $168|\beta| \le e^{-\mu}$. It does **not** certify 4D continuum Yang–Mills theory, a continuum limit, weak coupling, boundary conditions other than free ones, gauge invariance of the state, the Hilbert-space completion of the Osterwalder–Schrader form or a self-adjoint transfer operator and Hamiltonian on it (10e and 10f are statements on the pre-Hilbert space of local observables), a string tension defined as a limit or any lower bound on Wilson loops, Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction of a continuum theory, or the Clay Millennium Prize Problem. That the Hopf-coordinate measure of Target 8 is the Haar measure of $SU(2)$ is the classical volume formula of $S^3$; its bi-invariance is **not** formalized (8a and 8b do not depend on it; Target 10 uses only the invariance of the Hopf grids under inversion, which is proved).

## Critical Mathematical Note on Target 3 (`n!` Ordered-Cluster Caveat)

Independent audit (2026-11-24) identified a crucial mathematical limitation in the legacy `rg/cluster_expansion.v` + `ym/small_field.v` formulation captured by Target 3 (`Challenge_MasterConditionalStatement`):
- `clusters_Λ_N` enumerates **ordered** NoDup lists of length $\le N_{\max}$ without a $\frac{1}{|X|!}$ symmetry factor, and Target 3 sets `ursell := 1`.
- On an $n$-vertex path with $\beta = 86, a = 1$, each vertex has $\le 3$ overlapping neighbors, so both KP conditions hold ($3 e^{-7.6} \le 1$, $3 e^{-6.6} \le 1$), yet all $n!$ permutations of the path connect its endpoints and contribute $n! e^{-7.6 n} \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$, violating the pinned bound $\le e^2$ for large $n$.
- **Resolution in Targets 5a–5c:** Targets 5a–5c implement the standard ordered-tuple Mayer / Kotecký–Preiss expansion (Fernández–Procacci 2007 §2; Kotecký–Preiss 1986 §2): ordered tuples $X \in \Lambda^n$ (repetitions allowed), genuine $1/n!$ symmetry factor, and the genuine Ursell coefficient $\varphi^T(X) = \sum_{H \subseteq \mathcal{G}(X)\text{ conn.}} (-1)^{|E(H)|}$. In this formulation, the pinned exponential-moment bounds and correlator exponential decay are **proved unconditionally from the elementary single-polymer KP inequality** via Penrose's (1967) BFS tree-graph inequality and rooted-tree induction — with zero analytic assumptions.

### What Target 7 does and does not establish

- It concerns the lattice gauge theory with the **finite abelian** gauge group $\mathbb{Z}_2$, in **finite volume** with free boundary conditions, at **strong coupling** only ($|\beta| \le 1/168$ in 7b, $|\tanh\beta| \le 1/189$ in 7d, $|\tanh\beta| \le 1/63$ in 7e). All bounds are uniform in the volume, but no infinite-volume object is constructed.
- 7b is exponential clustering of a truncated correlation function. No transfer matrix, no reflection positivity and no spectral (mass) gap of a Hamiltonian is formalized, and nothing is said about a continuum limit.
- 7e is an **upper** bound for **rectangular** Wilson loops whose links all lie inside the dynamical volume. 8b. **`ChallengeSU2Gauge.Challenge_SU2Gauge4D_WilsonLoopAreaLaw`** *(strong-coupling Wilson-loop area law, an upper bound — **0 analytic hypotheses, 0 combinatorial hypotheses**, 0 project axioms)*
   For every non-empty finite list $G$ of unit quaternions that is invariant, as a multiset, under the central element $-1$ (`Permutation (map qneg G) G`), every $\beta$ (of either sign) with $63\,(e^{2|\beta|} - 1) \le 1$, every finite `NoDup` volume $\Lambda$ of canonical $\mathbb{Z}^4$ plaquettes, every corner $s$, all directions $\mu \ne \nu$ and all side lengths $a, b$ such that **every link of the rectangular loop $C$ belongs to `z2_links Lambda`**:

   uniformly in $\Lambda$ **and in $G$**. As in Target 7e the hypothesis on the loop links cannot be dropped.
   *Submitted proof* (`ym/su2_area_law.v`), with $\delta = e^{2|\beta|} - 1$ and $P_p = \frac12 \mathrm{tr}\, U_p$: (i) *Mayer expansion*: $e^{\beta P_p + |\beta|} = 1 + r_p$ with $0 \le r_p \le \delta$, so the expectation is $N/Z$ with $N = \sum_{S \subseteq \Lambda} \langle W \prod_{p \in S} r_p \rangle_0$ and $Z = \langle \prod_{p \in \Lambda} (1 + r_p) \rangle_0$, where $\langle\cdot\rangle_0$ is the a-priori average. (iii) *Center symmetry*: write $r_p = r_p^+ + r_p^-$ (even and odd parts under $U_p \to -U_p$) and expand over the subsets $R \subseteq T$ carrying the odd parts. Multiplying **one** link variable by $-1$ preserves the a-priori measure (`csum_flip`) and multiplies the term of $R$ by $(-1)^{k}$, $k$ = number of occurrences of the link in $C$ and in the boundaries of the plaquettes of $R$; hence the term vanishes unless the mod-2 boundary of $R$ is $C$. ### What Target 8 does and does not establish

- It concerns the **lattice** gauge theory with the non-abelian compact simple gauge group $SU(2)$ and the Wilson action, in **finite volume** with free boundary conditions, at **strong coupling** only ($|\beta| \le 1/168$ in 8a/8d, $e^{2|\beta|} - 1 \le 1/63$ in 8b/8e). - Strong coupling (small $\beta$) is the opposite of the regime $\beta \to \infty$ in which a continuum limit would have to be taken. Nothing here bears on the continuum limit, and Target 8 is **not** a step that could be continued to the Clay Millennium Prize Problem by the same method.

That this image measure is the Haar measure of $SU(2)$ is the classical volume formula of $S^3$; its invariance under left and right translations is **not** formalized here. Targets 8a and 8b do not depend on this identification: they hold for every finite a-priori distribution of the stated kind, uniformly, and therefore for every weak limit of such distributions.

### What Target 10 does and does not establish

- It is a statement about the **lattice** theory at **strong coupling** ($|\beta| \le 1/43008$). Nothing here bears on the continuum limit or on weak coupling, where the physical mass gap problem lives; the rate $m = \mu/2$ ($\ge \ln 16$ throughout $43008\,|\beta| \le 1$, and $m = \frac12 \ln\frac{1}{168|\beta|}$ for $\beta \ne 0$) is a lower bound for the mass gap in lattice units and is not sharp.
- 10a is independence of the exhausting sequence of finite volumes with **free** boundary conditions. Other boundary conditions, and uniqueness of the Gibbs state in the sense of the DLR equations, are **not** formalized.
- 10b is invariance under the translation by one lattice unit in the positive direction of each axis (hence under all their iterates) and under the time reflection. Invariance under the inverse translations follows by applying 10b to the inversely translated observable, but is not stated separately; lattice rotations and the reflections in the other axes are **not** formalized.
- 10c–10f concern the real pre-Hilbert space of bounded uniformly continuous observables localized on finitely many plaquettes at non-negative times, with the positive semidefinite form $(F, G) = \langle \Theta F \cdot G \rangle$. 10e and 10f say that $T^k$ has norm at most $e^{-mk}$ on the orthogonal complement of the vacuum in that space. The physical Hilbert space (the quotient by the null vectors and its completion), the transfer operator as a bounded self-adjoint operator on it, and the Hamiltonian $-\ln T$ are **not** constructed: the extension of the bound 10f from the dense subspace to the completion is standard functional analysis and is **not** formalized. Positivity of $T$ (which needs reflection positivity with respect to hyperplanes through the midpoints of the time-like links) is **not** proved either, so the statement is about $|(\varphi, T^k \psi)|$.
- The observables of 10c–10f are **not** required to be gauge invariant; the gauge-invariant ones (Wilson loops, plaquette variables and their functions) form a subspace, to which the bounds apply. Gauge invariance of the state is **not** formalized.
- Beyond the convergence of the grid averages (Target 8c), the proofs use the Hopf grids only through their invariance under inversion (`grid_conj`), which is proved; bi-invariance of the limiting measure is **not** formalized.
- The mathematics is classical (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; Seiler 1982). What is new here is the machine-checked formalization with zero project axioms.
